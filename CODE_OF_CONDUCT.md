# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors and participants in the `kktypr` project, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Expected Standards

We expect all contributors to adhere to the following standards:

- **Constructive Communication**: Express differing viewpoints with patience and professional respect. Focus on the code, architecture, and ergonomics.
- **Empathy and Collaboration**: Welcome newcomers and encourage diverse perspectives across developer tooling and learning methods.
- **Accountability**: Gracefully accept constructive technical feedback, acknowledge errors, and work collectively toward higher code quality.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, or repository channels):

- Harassment, trolling, insults, or derogatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing private personal information of others without permission.
- Disruptive or bad-faith behavior in code reviews or project discussions.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate or harmful.
